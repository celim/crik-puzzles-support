Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings > Purchases > Restore, or tap "Restore purchases" on the Full Puzzle screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

What does Full Puzzle unlock?
Full Puzzle is a one-time purchase that unlocks the NORMAL and HARD boards in all 8 puzzles and adds 50 hints. It is not a subscription.

What is the Hint Pack?
Each Hint Pack purchase adds 50 hints you can use in any puzzle.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?
No. The app works fully offline and does not need an account.

Is my progress saved?
Yes, progress and settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
