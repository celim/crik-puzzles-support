Privacy Policy

Crik Puzzles - Last updated: October 17, 2026

Crik Puzzles ("the app") is developed by Chaieun Lim (Crik Mobile). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your data to our servers or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, settings and hint count are stored only on your device. We cannot access this data. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email chaieun.lim@gmail.com.
